# Privacy Policy — Maisha

**Last updated:** 7 September 2026

Maisha (“the app”) is a single-player life simulator. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

## Data we store on your device

Maisha saves gameplay on your device only:

- Character lives, family, jobs, assets, and related progress in three save slots
- Achievements and settings such as language, sound, haptics, and notifications

This data stays on the device unless you share a life card, reset data in Settings, or uninstall the app.

## Ads

The app may show ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect advertising identifiers and approximate location or device information as described in [Google’s advertising privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can reset your advertising ID in Android settings. We do not sell personal information.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Maisha may send local reminders about your saved lives. These are scheduled on the device and are not sent from our servers.

## Sharing

If you use Share My Life, Maisha creates an image on your device and opens the Android share sheet. We do not upload that image to our own servers.

## Children

Maisha is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads, if enabled, use AdMob’s configured content rating.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the [Maisha GitHub repository](https://github.com/stewiriffin/Life-simulator).
